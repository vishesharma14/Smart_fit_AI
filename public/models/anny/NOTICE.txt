This file was produced by SizerAI's tools/anny-export/export_anny_compact.py: it contains a
principal-component approximation of Anny rest-pose meshes (not Anny's original data files),
the mesh triangle list, vertex masks and landmark indices. The non-commercial SMPL-X topology
distributed separately by NAVER is not used.
